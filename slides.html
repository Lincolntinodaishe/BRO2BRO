Pulse

The barbershop got men talking.
We built what comes next.

An AI-powered wellness companion that connects Black men in Arkansas to preventive health, mental wellness, and community support — starting where trust already exists.

The Problem

60% of Black men in Arkansas
have high blood pressure.
Half don't know it.

System Distrust

Historical trauma with healthcare institutions creates real barriers to seeking care.

Access Gaps

Rural and low-income communities lack nearby clinics that feel culturally safe.

The Silence

Men are taught not to show weakness. That silence is killing us — literally.

Our Solution

Meet men where they already are.
The barbershop.

Pulse integrates with UAMS's 61 partner barbershops across Arkansas — places Black men already trust — and extends that trust into real, accessible health care through AI, maps, mentors, and community.

How It Works

From the chair to real care

1

Barbershop Entry

Scan a QR code at any UAMS partner barbershop — or download the app. No forms, no pressure, no appointment needed.

2

Talk to Pulse AI

Pulse uses motivational interviewing to check in on your health, stress, and life — and figures out what kind of support you actually need.

3

Get Connected

Free clinics, appointments, peer mentors, or the community forum — everything in one place, all built for you.

Core Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Pulse AI Chat

An AI wellness companion trained in motivational interviewing. Checks in on BP, stress, sleep, and mood. End-to-end encrypted.

Claude API · 24/7

Resource Finder

Free clinics, partner barbershops, mental health providers, and support groups — filtered by zip code and sorted by distance.

Google Maps API

Peer Mentor Network

Connect with Black men who've navigated diabetes, depression, recovery, fatherhood. Real people, not clinicians. Community-verified.

Live Matching

Appointments + Community

Book clinic visits, barbershop screenings, or virtual counseling. Share milestones and stories in the private community forum.
