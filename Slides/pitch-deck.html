The Problem
4–6 YRS
The gap in life expectancy between Black men and white men — from conditions that are fully manageable.
  • Only 1 in 3 Black men controls his hypertension
  • 40% higher cardiovascular mortality vs. white men
  • 43% avoid healthcare due to deep-seated distrust
  • Arkansas ranks bottom-5 in US health outcomes
Why they stay away
Healthcare distrust
Mental health stigma
No culturally competent care
Rural access deserts

Why Now
The barbershop already works. We just scaled it.
  • Most trusted institution in Black male culture — more than a clinic
  • AI can now deliver that conversation 24/7, in natural language
  • SMS-first design means it works on any phone, anywhere in rural AR
  • 2025 JMIR review: AI motivational interviewing is feasible & effective

Our Solution
Bro2Bro: The barbershop, in your pocket, 24/7.
Free for users. Culturally built. Privacy-first. Powered by AI.
🗣️
TALK
Bro.AI: judgment-free, 24/7 wellness chat. No clinical jargon. Motivational interviewing style. 988 crisis escalation built in.
🗺️
CONNECT
Find free clinics, book appointments via FHIR API, and match with screened peer mentors — all inside the app.
👥
CREW
Accountability groups: shared 90-day health journey, BP milestones, streak tracking, family sharing.
Peer mentors share personal experience — they do not give medical advice. Screened, trained in scope boundaries, covered under platform terms of service.
